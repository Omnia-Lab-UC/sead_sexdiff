# Proposal

## Why

The primary analysis (`reports/motco-astrocyte-2026-10-19.md`), the forced-rank sweep (`reports/motco-astrocyte-rank-sensitivity-2026-10-19.md`) and the chrX variant (`reports/motco-astrocyte-chrx-sensitivity-2026-10-19.md`) all measure the sex × ADNC-stage comparison in a latent space whose first PLS component is, by an exploratory check made on 2026-10-19 from the kept exports and analyses, a donor-level composition axis rather than a stage axis: it correlates −0.73 with the RNA TMM factor and −0.82 with a ribosomal-protein / ferritin score, against +0.49 with stage; a third of the kept RNA genes correlate above 0.5 with that score and its strongest carriers after the ribosomal genes are short abundant cytoplasmic transcripts (`FAU`, `UQCR10`, `COX7C`, `ATP5ME`, `MRPL40`); dropping the twelve ribosomal and ferritin genes leaves the axis intact; PLS components nest, so every forced rank of the sweep kept that axis as its first and largest component; and the six analysis covariates leave the partial correlation at −0.81. The two sexes' net displacements along that axis (about 33 against 18 units) are what `delta` measured. Those checks live in a session scratchpad: nothing in the repository reproduces them, no donor-level technical variable other than nuclei counts, RIN and PMI exists, and nothing measures whether the composition axis is a donor-level property of the preparation (ambient or cytoplasmic RNA) or varies with astrocyte state within donors. The feature-selection and residualization variants the team wants to run (the next change) depend on both: which technical covariates exist, and whether regressing them out removes noise or biology. This change produces the measurements and the covariates, and nothing else.

## What Changes

- **New `technical-covariates` step** (cluster login node; needs the raw donor objects and `results/pseudobulk/`). For every RNA donor object it selects the astrocyte nuclei exactly as the pseudobulk step does (taxonomy mapping, `Reference` donors excluded, ≥ `RNA_MIN_GENES` genes) and reads their rows of the `UMIs` layer and their `obs` QC columns, never the normalized `X`. Per nucleus it computes the UMI fraction in each predeclared gene set, `TECHNICAL_GENE_SETS` (ribosomal protein genes by name pattern, mitochondrially encoded `MT-` genes, hemoglobin genes), and reads `Fraction mitochondrial UMIs`, `Number of UMIs`, `Genes detected`, `Doublet score` and the library-level 10x metrics (`GEX_Reads_mapped_confidently_to_intronic_regions`, `GEX_Fraction_of_transcriptomic_reads_in_cells`, `GEX_Median_UMI_counts_per_cell`, `GEX_sequencing_saturation`, `load_name`). Per donor it writes the nucleus means, the UMI-weighted pseudobulk fractions (recomputed from `results/pseudobulk/rna_counts.h5ad` and checked against the per-nucleus sums), the within-donor SD of each fraction, the within-donor share of its variance explained by `Supertype`, and the number of libraries. From the ATAC objects it reads the library-level `ATAC_*` metrics (TSS enrichment, fraction of fragments in peaks, fraction in cells, percent duplicates, median fragments per nucleus) for the astrocyte nuclei. It writes `results/pseudobulk/donor_technical.csv`, `nucleus_technical_summary.csv` and `technical_manifest.json`; it does **not** touch `donor_covariates.csv` or `covariates_manifest.json`, whose hashes the primary export's reference check binds. Resumable per donor, like the pseudobulk.
- **New `axis-diagnostics` step** (any workstation; needs `results/motco_input/`, `results/motco_analysis/`, `results/pseudobulk/donor_technical.csv` and, when present, `results/motco_rank_sensitivity/`). Per cohort it writes `results/motco_diagnostics/<cohort>/`: Spearman correlations of every latent component of the primary analysis (and of each forced-rank analysis found) with the technical variables, the TMM factors and library sizes from the export manifest, the six analysis covariates, stage and sex, plus the partial correlations given the six analysis covariates; the same for the first three principal components of each z-scored block; the share of the squared sex-difference and net-displacement geometry along each component from `stage_means.csv`; the share of kept features whose correlation with the first component exceeds `DIAGNOSTIC_CORRELATION_THRESHOLD`; and the feature-selection accounting (quantiles of kept features' mean log2-CPM, the share below 0 and 1, the share of total abundance-passing variance held by the kept features, the variance at the cut) from the export's `features_<omic>.csv`. Tables as CSV, a Markdown rendering, a manifest with every input hash. Nothing from this step feeds a preprocessing choice: it reads labels, so it is a diagnostic, never a filter.
- **The pseudobulk's nucleus selection becomes a shared helper** used by both the pseudobulk and the technical step, so "the same nuclei" is a property of the code; `pseudobulk` output stays byte-identical (checked against the per-donor cache on the cluster).
- **A committed report** `reports/motco-astrocyte-technical-axis-<date>.md` with both cohorts' diagnostics and the donor-level technical summary pasted from the generated files, read with fixed rules: a latent axis that correlates with a technical variable is a measurement-space finding about the primary analysis, not a reinterpretation of its statistics; a composition axis that is donor-level (high between-donor share, low within-donor supertype share) is read as a preparation property and a candidate for residualization, one that varies with supertype within donors is read as cell state and not residualized; the primary analysis stays primary and its non-rejections keep their reading rules.
- README and AGENTS.md document both commands, the constants and the reading rules; the cluster section says where each runs.

Out of scope: any change to the primary export, analysis, rank sweep or `keep_chrx` variant (all read, hashed, never rerun); any new export variant (the next change, `mtg-astrocyte-feature-selection-sensitivity`, takes the technical table as input); any MOTCO change; a verdict on whether the primary result is wrong.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `donor-technical-table`: the per-donor technical quality table of the astrocyte nuclei (gene-set UMI fractions, nucleus and library QC, within-donor dispersion and supertype share), produced from the raw donor objects and the pseudobulk, kept apart from the donor covariate table, with its manifest.
- `latent-axis-diagnostic`: the reproducible measurement of what the MOTCO latent axes and the exported blocks' main axes correlate with (technical, covariate and design variables), the geometry shares along components, the feature-selection accounting, their rendering and the committed report that reads them.

## Impact

- `src/sead_sexdiff/data.py`: a shared `select_astrocyte_nuclei` helper extracted from `pseudobulk_donor` (output-preserving); new constants `TECHNICAL_GENE_SETS`, `TECHNICAL_OBS_COLUMNS`, `TECHNICAL_OUTPUTS`, `DIAGNOSTIC_VARIABLES`, `DIAGNOSTIC_CORRELATION_THRESHOLD`, `DIAGNOSTIC_OUTPUTS`; new functions `technical_donor`, `run_technical_covariates`, `run_axis_diagnostics`, `render_axis_diagnostics`.
- `src/sead_sexdiff/cli.py`: `technical-covariates` (`--omic`, `--data-root`, `--pseudobulk-dir`, `--out-dir`, `--force`) and `axis-diagnostics` (`--cohort`, `--input-dir`, `--analysis-dir`, `--rank-dir`, `--pseudobulk-dir`, `--out-dir`, `--force`).
- `scripts/`: `technical.sbatch` mirroring `pseudobulk.sbatch` for the RNA read (the astrocyte rows of the `UMIs` layer, the same read the pseudobulk makes), and `sync_pseudobulk.sh` extended to pull the three new files (they live in `results/pseudobulk/`).
- `reports/`: one new committed report. `results/` stays gitignored.
- No dependency change: h5py, anndata, numpy, pandas and scipy are installed.
- Compute: the RNA technical read is the pseudobulk's per-donor read (84 objects, resumable); the ATAC read is `obs` only; the diagnostics are seconds per cohort.
- Scientific choices introduced here (flag and ask before changing): `TECHNICAL_GENE_SETS` (ribosomal protein genes matching `^RP[LS]\d+[A-Z]?$`, `^RPLP[0-2]$` or `RPSA`, so pseudogenes are excluded; the 13 `MT-` genes; `HBB`, `HBA1`, `HBA2`), `TECHNICAL_OBS_COLUMNS`, nucleus-weighted means beside UMI-weighted pseudobulk fractions, the within-donor supertype share as the biology-versus-preparation statistic, `DIAGNOSTIC_CORRELATION_THRESHOLD` (0.3, a reporting flag, not an adoption rule: adoption rules belong to the next change and are written there before its runs), and the rule that the diagnostic never feeds a filter.
