# Proposal

## Why

The primary export keeps the 5,000 most variable log2-CPM features per omic, and the diagnostic change (`mtg-astrocyte-technical-axis-diagnostic`, which this change requires archived) makes reproducible what a scratch check found on 2026-10-19: raw log-CPM variance favours low-count features (55% of kept RNA genes below about 2 CPM, the ATAC top 5,000 holding 7.7% of the abundance-passing variance at a nearly flat cut), the dominant axis of the kept RNA block is a donor-level composition axis carried by ribosomal-protein, ferritin and short abundant cytoplasmic transcripts, that axis is the first and largest PLS component of the primary analysis and of every forced rank, and neither the six analysis covariates (applied by MOTCO only in the regression on the latent scores, never in the latent fit) nor removing twelve ribosomal genes removes it. The chrX variant (`reports/motco-astrocyte-chrx-sensitivity-2026-10-19.md`) moved nothing because it changed nothing about that axis. The registry `PREPROCESSING_VARIANTS` was designed to take further variants, and the decision of 2026-10-19 to defer residualized features rested on the covariate handling inside MOTCO being sufficient, which the diagnostic shows it is not for the latent fit. This change adds the feature-content variants as predeclared registry entries, run and compared exactly as `keep_chrx` was, plus one composed candidate and a written adoption rule, so that any revision of the primary preprocessing is decided by a rule fixed before the runs, never by p-values.

## What Changes

- **Four single-setting variants** in `PREPROCESSING_VARIANTS`, each the primary export with one setting changed and the analysis unchanged:
  - `exclude_families`: before the abundance filter, remove RNA genes in `TECHNICAL_GENE_SETS` (`ribosomal`, `mitochondrial`, `hemoglobin`; the diagnostic change's constant), status `excluded_gene_set`; ATAC unchanged.
  - `rna_trend_ratio`: rank RNA features at the selection step by the ratio of their log2-CPM variance to a mean–variance trend (the median variance of twenty equal-count bins of mean log2-CPM, linearly interpolated) instead of by raw variance; ATAC unchanged.
  - `atac_mean_accessibility`: rank ATAC peaks at the selection step by mean log2-CPM (total accessibility) instead of by variance; RNA unchanged.
  - `residualized`: after log2-CPM and before the selection step, replace each abundance-passing feature's values by the residuals of its regression on an intercept and the technical set `RESIDUALIZATION_COVARIATES`, plus the feature mean, so units stay log2-CPM; the selection then ranks residual variance. The technical set is the per-omic log10 library size and the gene-set pseudobulk fractions of `donor_technical.csv`, minus any fraction the technical-axis report classifies as cell state under its fixed rule. Sex, stage and the analysis covariates never enter it. The design matrix is written beside the blocks; `covariates.csv` stays byte-identical to the primary's.
- **One composed candidate**, `revised_candidate`: the four settings together, in the primary's order (families removed → abundance → TMM → log2-CPM → residualized → ranked by trend ratio / mean accessibility).
- **A predeclared adoption rule**, `ADOPTION_RULE`, evaluated by the step from generated files and written into `comparison/<cohort>/adoption_check.csv` for the candidate: in both cohorts, no latent component of the candidate's CV-selected analysis has an absolute Spearman correlation at or above `DIAGNOSTIC_CORRELATION_THRESHOLD` with any variable of `ADOPTION_CHECK_VARIABLES`, the technical variables that are not regressors (the TMM factors, the nucleus QC means, RIN, PMI); p-values, rejection statuses and CV AUROC are not part of the rule. If it holds, a later change may propose the candidate as a revised primary with the current primary kept as the reference; if it does not, the variants stay sensitivity analyses.
- **The comparison gains per-variant accounting**: features removed per gene set, the selection rule per omic and the kept features' mean log2-CPM quantiles, the residualization's covariates and per-feature R² quantiles, beside the shared / only-here counts the step already writes; the chrX items stay for `keep_chrx`. One report, `reports/motco-astrocyte-feature-selection-<date>.md`, reads every variant as sensitivity, attributes the change in the first latent axis to each setting, and states the adoption check's outcome.
- `preprocessing-sensitivity` gains the inputs the new variants need (`donor_technical.csv` and its manifest, checked by hash against the pseudobulk manifest the export reads) and refuses a residualized variant when a cohort donor lacks a technical value.

Out of scope: rerunning or modifying the primary export, analysis, rank sweep or `keep_chrx` (read and checked, as before); other `N` values; the forced-rank sweep on a variant; any MOTCO change (covariate residualization inside MOTCO's latent fit is proposed in the MOTCO repository separately); the revised-primary change itself.

### Modified Capabilities
- `motco-preprocessing-sensitivity`: the registry grows from one to six entries, a variant may change one predeclared setting among the excluded gene sets, the selection rule per omic and the residualization, or compose them as the candidate; the inputs include the technical table; the feature accounting becomes variant-aware; the comparison carries the adoption check for the candidate; the report requirements extend to the new variants and the adoption rule.

## Impact

- `src/sead_sexdiff/data.py`: `preprocess_block` and `_export_cohort` take `excluded_gene_sets`, `selection_rule` (per omic) and `residualize` (design matrix) keywords, defaulting to the primary's behaviour so `export` output stays byte-identical; a `trend_ratio` ranking helper; a residualization helper; `PREPROCESSING_VARIANTS` extended; `RESIDUALIZATION_COVARIATES`, `ADOPTION_CHECK_VARIABLES`, `ADOPTION_RULE` constants; `_check_technical_reference`; `_feature_accounting` and `render_preprocessing_sensitivity` made variant-aware; the adoption check reusing the diagnostic change's correlation functions.
- `src/sead_sexdiff/cli.py`: `--variant` accepts the new names and `all`; no new flags.
- `reports/`: one new committed report. `results/` stays gitignored.
- No dependency change (numpy, pandas, scipy present).
- Compute: five variants × two cohorts, one export (seconds) and one CV analysis (about two minutes) each, on the workstation that holds the kept outputs and the synced `results/pseudobulk/` with `donor_technical.csv`.
- Depends on `mtg-astrocyte-technical-axis-diagnostic` being archived: `TECHNICAL_GENE_SETS`, `donor_technical.csv`, the correlation functions, `DIAGNOSTIC_CORRELATION_THRESHOLD` and the technical-axis report's cell-state classification.
- Scientific choices introduced here (flag and ask before changing): the four variants and the candidate as registry entries; the trend definition (twenty equal-count bins, median variance, linear interpolation, ratio statistic, ties by name); mean accessibility as the ATAC rule; the residualization position (after log2-CPM, before selection) and set (`RESIDUALIZATION_COVARIATES`, with the cell-state exclusion rule); keeping the analysis covariates unchanged in the variant analyses (double adjustment of library-size proxies accepted and stated); `ADOPTION_RULE` and `ADOPTION_CHECK_VARIABLES`; `n_jobs = 1` for reported results; the rule that the primary analysis stays primary and that the adoption check never reads p-values.
