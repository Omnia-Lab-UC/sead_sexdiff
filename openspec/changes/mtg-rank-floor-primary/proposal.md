# Proposal

## Why

Every primary analysis of the case study selected latent rank 1 (astrocyte `cohort_30` / `cohort_50`, microglia `cohort_30` / `cohort_50`, CV mean AUROC 0.63–0.69). In a one-dimensional latent space the two sexes' net displacements are parallel or antiparallel, so `angle` is 0° or 180° by construction and its RRPP null is a two-point distribution: the primary `angle` (p = 1.0 in both astrocyte cohorts) and `shape` carried no orientation information at all, which the reports could only state as a limitation. MOTCO resolved this on its side (`minimum-latent-rank`, MOTCO 0.10.0, tagged `v0.10.0` on 2026-10-26): the production latent space now selects the rank from candidates at or above a **floor of 2** (`TrajectoryAnalysisParams.min_components = 2`, the new default), after a predeclared size check at the Phase 6 SEA-AD design, weakened to this case study's AUROC regime, returned `adopt_floor` at both strengths (floor-2 anchor `delta` 0.042 / 0.044, `angle` 0.054 / 0.060, `shape` 0.036 / 0.038, translation control likewise within the 0.0695 bound; `MOTCO docs/reports/rank-floor-2026-10-23.md`). MOTCO's roadmap (Next changes item 4) hands the follow-up to this repository: rerun the cohorts at the floor in their own proposal, pinning 0.10.0.

This change makes the floored analysis **the primary analysis** of both cell types and re-issues every result that depends on a MOTCO analysis, so no output of the case study mixes the two rank rules. The floor is chosen by MOTCO's own validation, before any floored run on these data, not from these results.

Known when this change was written, and stated so: the astrocyte forced-rank sweep (MOTCO 0.8.0, `reports/motco-astrocyte-rank-sensitivity-2026-10-19.md`) rejected nothing at any forced rank 2–12 in either cohort; and PLS components nest, so the first latent component `pls_0` is identical at every rank (recorded by `axis-diagnostics` on the sweep). The predeclared reading below does not depend on either.

## What Changes

- **Pin MOTCO `v0.10.0`** (`uv add "motco @ git+https://github.com/tomszar/MOTCO@v0.10.0"`; scikit-learn stays at MOTCO's exact pin). `ANALYSIS_MOTCO_MIN_VERSION` becomes `0.10.0`, so no analysis can run without the floor.
- **A recorded rank floor**: a new constant `ANALYSIS_RANK_FLOOR = 2`, passed explicitly as `min_components` in every `TrajectoryAnalysisParams` the package builds (`analyze`, `rank-sensitivity`, `preprocessing-sensitivity`, `latent-sensitivity`, `exclusion-sensitivity`), checked at run time to equal MOTCO's default (MOTCO's defaults are not overridden: if they ever diverge the run stops), recorded in every analysis manifest and in `cli_equivalent` (`--min-components 2`), and printed in `summary.md` beside the CV-selected rank. `_check_reference` additionally checks that the reference analysis was made at the floor, so no sensitivity, diagnostic or stage-DE step can compare against an unfloored primary. The rank-1 findings and notes stay (they fire for an analysis at floor 1, which `motco analyze --min-components 1` can still produce).
- **A same-machine pin-move gate before any floored run**, as at the 0.8.0 → 0.9.0 move: on the workstation, the four primary analyses (two cell types × two cohorts) run under MOTCO 0.10.0 with the floor set to 1 and, from the pre-change commit, under MOTCO 0.9.0; their CSVs must be byte-identical and their JSON equal apart from runtimes, times, revisions and versions. If the gate fails the change stops. The comparison with the kept directories is recorded, not a gate (the astrocyte primary was made on the cluster).
- **Superseded outputs are moved, not deleted**: before the reruns, every MOTCO-dependent output directory under `results/` and `results/microglia/` moves to `results/superseded/mtg-rank-floor-primary/` with its relative path kept, on the workstation and on the cluster, with sha256 checked before and after the move, so every hash cited by an earlier report stays resolvable. The MOTCO-independent outputs (`pseudobulk/`, `motco_input/`, the donor, covariate, technical and flag tables, the annotation) are not touched and hash unchanged at the end.
- **Every MOTCO-dependent step is re-run under the floor**, from scratch, in dependency order, with the commands of the earlier reports and `n_jobs = 1`:
  - astrocytes: `analyze`; `rank-sensitivity`; `axis-diagnostics` (with the sweep); `preprocessing-sensitivity --variant all` (all eight variants, including both trend-normalized candidates, the adoption check and the adoption precedence) and `axis-diagnostics` on each variant root; `latent-sensitivity` and `axis-diagnostics` on the primary and both arms; `stage-de --omic both` and `stage-de-limma --omic both`; `exclusion-sensitivity` with `axis-diagnostics`, `stage-de --omic both` and `stage-de-limma --omic rna` on the variant;
  - microglia: `analyze`; `axis-diagnostics`; `latent-sensitivity` and `axis-diagnostics` on both arms; `stage-de --omic both` and `stage-de-limma --omic both`; `exclusion-sensitivity` with its diagnostics and stage DE as above; `positive-control` on both bases (`primary`, `latent_adjusted`).
  The stage-DE fits do not depend on MOTCO; they are refitted anyway so every output is made under one pin, and their tables are compared with the superseded ones (recorded, not a gate).
- **A version-consistency check at the end**: every manifest under `results/` outside `results/superseded/` that records a MOTCO version records `0.10.0`, and every analysis record carries the floor.
- **Predeclared reading**, written here before any floored run:
  - The floored analysis is the primary of each cell type; the unfloored analyses are superseded records, not a sensitivity arm, and are not cited as results. `cohort_30` stays primary and `cohort_50` sensitivity.
  - The binding rules hold unchanged: the CV-selected rank (now ≥ 2) beside `angle`; `angle` and `shape` non-rejections uninformative; a `delta` rejection a difference, not specifically of size. The floor defines `angle` and `shape` in at least two dimensions; it does not make them powerful (MOTCO's check found power at the null at its strengths), so the report gives each analysis's `config_spectrum` eigengap and null summary as MOTCO advises.
  - A rejection status that differs between a floored analysis and its superseded unfloored one is reported beside it as dependence on the rank rule; the floored status is the primary result, read under the binding rules, and nothing is re-tuned.
  - Every sensitivity reading (rank, preprocessing, latent adjustment, donor exclusion, stage DE) is re-read against the floored primary under its own unchanged rules; their registries, expectations and adoption rules are not changed.
  - Expectations from the construction, checked, a difference recorded as a finding: `pls_0` (and so E3, the exclusion axis check and the flag check's `pls_0` AUC) reproduces the superseded values; the stage-DE tables, counts and E1 reproduce; the forced run at the CV-selected rank equals the floored primary (the sweep's existing check).
  - The positive control's expectations E1–E3 are unchanged; their outcome on the floored primary is the primary-basis outcome from this change on, and the superseded outcome is reported beside it. The restated control on `latent_adjusted` keeps its predeclared reading.
- **One committed report**, `reports/motco-rank-floor-primary-<date>.md`, covering both cell types: provenance and the pin-move gate; the floored primary analyses in full (as the case-study report requires); per re-issued step, its regenerated comparison tables and an outcome table "superseded → floored" (rank, CV AUROC, statistics, p-values, rejections, adoption check and precedence, concordance cells, E1–E3 and the control outcome on both bases, axis and flag checks); the reading above in the outcome that occurred; limitations. Numbers pasted from generated files and checked by a script. The earlier reports stay as dated records, unchanged.
- README and AGENTS.md: the pin, the floor constant, the superseded layout and the reading.

Out of scope: any change to `ANALYSIS_FORCED_RANKS`, to a registry (`PREPROCESSING_VARIANTS`, `LATENT_SENSITIVITY_ARMS`, `DONOR_EXCLUSION_VARIANTS`, `STAGE_DE_MODELS`, `POSITIVE_CONTROL_*`), to an expectation, adoption rule or threshold; MOTCO's block weighting (roadmap item 5); `rank-sensitivity` or `preprocessing-sensitivity` on microglia (astrocyte-only steps stay astrocyte-only); MOTCO-independent steps (`pseudobulk`, `covariates`, `technical-covariates`, `donor-flags`, `export`), which are not rerun; edits to earlier reports.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `motco-release-migration`: how a MOTCO pin move that changes the measurement re-issues the case study: the same-machine gate, superseded outputs moved under `results/superseded/<change>/` on every machine, the dependency-ordered rerun of every MOTCO-dependent step for every cell type, the version-consistency check, the untouched MOTCO-independent outputs, and the re-issue report with superseded values beside the new ones and a reading written before the reruns.

### Modified Capabilities
- `motco-trajectory-analysis`: MOTCO ≥ 0.10.0; the rank floor `ANALYSIS_RANK_FLOOR = 2` among the fixed measurement settings, recorded and checked by every step that reads the primary as its reference; the summary and the report read the floored rank (eigengap and null summary beside `angle`).
- `motco-rank-sensitivity`: the reference check includes the rank floor; the manifest's finding for a CV-selected rank outside the ladder no longer assumes rank 1.
- `cell-type-positive-control`: the control's outcome on the floored primary replaces the superseded outcome as the primary-basis outcome, with the superseded outcome reported beside it.

## Impact

- `pyproject.toml`, `uv.lock`: MOTCO `v0.10.0`.
- `src/sead_sexdiff/data.py`: `ANALYSIS_MOTCO_MIN_VERSION`, `ANALYSIS_RANK_FLOOR`, a check of MOTCO's default floor, `min_components` in the five `TrajectoryAnalysisParams` constructions, the manifest's `measurement`, `_cli_equivalent`, `render_analysis_summary`, `_check_reference`, the rank-sweep finding text. No CLI flag is added (the floor is a constant, never a flag).
- `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`.
- `results/` (gitignored), on `nixos-home` and the cluster: superseded outputs moved, every MOTCO-dependent output re-made. Compute on `nixos-home`, `n_jobs = 1`: about 50 CV analyses of 1–2 min and 12 forced ones of seconds, sixteen variant exports, eight stage-DE and eight limma runs of minutes; a few hours in all.
- `reports/`: one new report.
- Scientific choices introduced here (flag and ask before changing): the floored analysis as primary for both cell types; `ANALYSIS_RANK_FLOOR = 2` tied to MOTCO's validated default; re-issuing every MOTCO-dependent output rather than keeping mixed pins; the superseded outputs as records, not a sensitivity arm; the predeclared reading above.
