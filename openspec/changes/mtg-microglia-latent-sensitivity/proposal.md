# Proposal

## Why

The microglia positive control did not hold (`reports/motco-microglia-positive-control-2026-10-22.md`): in `cohort_30` the stage signal is there (E1: 61 exported RNA stage genes against the astrocytes' 1) and the latent space separates the stages somewhat better (E2: CV AUROC 0.692 against 0.631), but the first and only latent component follows the donor's mitochondrial UMI fraction (|ρ| 0.844) more closely than stage (0.688), so E3 fails. By the predeclared rule the latent construction is in question for any cell type. The report could not say *why*: whether the stage-supervised basis cannot resolve stage on these blocks, or whether it resolves the one direction that stage and nucleus quality share, because in SEA-AD the two are entangled at the donor level (the mitochondrial fraction itself has Spearman 0.50 with stage among the microglia cohort donors, 0.31 among the astrocytes'; SEA-AD's own QC axis marks its most severe donors, Gabitto et al. 2024).

The astrocyte latent-adjustment sensitivity already built the tool that separates the two readings (`reports/motco-astrocyte-latent-adjustment-2026-10-21.md`): with MOTCO 0.9.0's `stage`-conditioned latent adjustment, the astrocyte first axis moved off the library-quality variables (largest technical |ρ| 0.38 against 0.84 for the primary) and onto stage (|ρ| 0.64–0.75 against 0.49–0.50). Running the same two predeclared arms on the microglia blocks, and restating the positive control on the adjusted basis with its expectations written before the run, tells whether the failed control is a failure of the latent construction or a consequence of the measured quality gradient. The arms stay sensitivity analyses: MOTCO's power comparison (`candidate_fails`) excludes the latent adjustment as a replacement of the primary, and nothing here changes the primary control's outcome or any astrocyte output or reading.

## What Changes

- **`latent-sensitivity` takes `--cell-type`** (default `astrocyte`, outputs byte-identical to today's), the ninth MTG command with the flag. It sets every default directory under the cell type's results root (`<root>/motco_input/`, `<root>/motco_analysis/`, `<root>/pseudobulk/`, `<root>/motco_latent_sensitivity/`); explicit directory flags still win. Every manifest the step reads (export, analysis, technical) must record the cell type or the step refuses it before writing anything (a pre-registry astrocyte manifest reads as astrocyte with a finding, as for the other readers); the arm manifests and the comparison manifest record it. The registry `LATENT_SENSITIVITY_ARMS`, the covariate set `LATENT_SENSITIVITY_COVARIATES`, the `stage` conditioning, the mediator check, `ANALYSIS_SEED`, `ANALYSIS_PERMUTATIONS` and `n_jobs = 1` for reported results are unchanged; `rank-sensitivity` and `preprocessing-sensitivity` keep no flag and stay astrocyte-only.
- **The two arms run on the microglia blocks**, both cohorts, on the unchanged microglia primary export with the microglia primary analysis as the read-only reference: `extended_covariates` (the six analysis covariates plus `ribosomal_pseudobulk` and `mitochondrial_pseudobulk` of the microglia technical table, covariate-blind basis) and `latent_adjusted` (the same eight, basis conditioned on `stage`). The mediator check is written before the arms; the microglia mitochondrial fraction is expected to be flagged (Spearman 0.50 with stage in `cohort_30`), and a flag changes no arm. `axis-diagnostics --cell-type microglia --analysis-dir` runs on both arms, into `results/microglia/motco_latent_sensitivity/diagnostics/<arm>/`, mirroring the astrocyte layout.
- **The positive control restated on the adjusted basis.** `positive-control` gains `--basis`, a name of the new registry `POSITIVE_CONTROL_BASES`: `primary` (the default; today's inputs, output path and bytes) and `latent_adjusted` (E2 and E3 read from the `latent_adjusted` arm of each cell type and from the diagnostic run on that arm, E1 unchanged because it is basis-independent, written to `results/microglia/positive_control/latent_adjusted/<cohort>/`). The expectations E1–E3, their comparisons, `POSITIVE_CONTROL_TECHNICAL_SOURCES` (the two adjusted fractions stay in the compared set) and the all-three rule in `POSITIVE_CONTROL_PRIMARY_COHORT` are not changed. `extended_covariates` gets no restated control: with the covariate-blind basis its latent space, CV rank and AUROC equal the primary's by MOTCO's contract, so E2 and E3 would repeat the primary's values; the report states and checks that equality. The chain is checked as for the primary basis: the arm manifests record the primary export's hashes and the primary analysis the arm was compared with, the diagnostic records the arm analysis it was made from, and every manifest records its root's cell type.
- **Predeclared reading of the restated control**, written here before any microglia arm runs and never changed from results. The primary-basis control failed on E3 in `cohort_30`. On the `latent_adjusted` basis in `cohort_30`:
  - if the control **holds**, the measured quality gradient explains the primary control's failure: once the library-quality direction is conditioned out of the basis, the pipeline resolves stage in the cell type with the stronger stage signal. This does not promote the arm (MOTCO's `candidate_fails` stands), does not change the primary-basis outcome, and does not change the astrocyte reading; it is the diagnosis a later MOTCO-side change (validation of the latent adjustment on blocks with a dominant nuisance axis) or a later revised-primary proposal would cite;
  - if it **does not hold**, the latent construction is in question beyond the quality gradient (composition of the `Micro-PVM_*` bundle, the rank-1 selection, the few microglia ATAC nuclei per donor are the stated candidates), and the diagnosis "technical gradient" is not supported;
  - E3 on the adjusted basis carries the mediator caveat in both directions: the adjustment removes the quality direction and, with it, any stage signal the fractions carry, so the stage |ρ| may rise or fall; a failure for that reason is still a failure, and the expectation is not re-tuned;
  - `cohort_50` is descriptive, as for the primary basis.
- **The microglia sex × stage arms** are a second set of sensitivity results, read under the latent-adjustment rules: a rejection status that differs is covariate-set dependence (primary vs `extended_covariates`) or basis dependence (`extended_covariates` vs `latent_adjusted`), never a result of the arm; a CV-selected rank that differs is reported beside `angle`; the non-rejection rules hold in every arm; the microglia primary analysis stays primary.
- **A committed report**, `reports/motco-microglia-latent-adjustment-<date>.md`: provenance, the mediator check, the comparison tables of both cohorts, the first-axis correlations of the primary and both arms, each `latent_adjusted` arm's `feature_r2` summary, the restated-control table beside the primary-basis table, the `extended_covariates` equality check, the reading rules and limitations.
- README and AGENTS.md document the flag, the basis registry, the restated control's reading rules and the layout.

Out of scope: the latent adjustment as a revised primary for either cell type (excluded by MOTCO's `candidate_fails`); `group_stage` conditioning; other covariate sets; `rank-sensitivity` or `preprocessing-sensitivity` on microglia; a minimum CV rank (a MOTCO change, roadmap item 4); any change to the expectations E1–E3, to the primary-basis outcome, or to any astrocyte output or reading.

### Modified Capabilities
- `motco-latent-adjustment-sensitivity`: the step runs per registered cell type (inputs under the cell type's root, cell type checked in every manifest read and recorded in every manifest written, outputs under the cell type's root); the committed report requirement becomes per cell type.
- `cell-type-positive-control`: the expectations are evaluated on a registered basis (`primary`, the default, unchanged; `latent_adjusted`, reading E2 and E3 from the arm analyses), with the predeclared reading of the restated control and the `extended_covariates` equality check.

## Impact

- Depends on `mtg-microglia-positive-control` (PR #12) being merged: the registry, `--cell-type`, the microglia results root and the `positive-control` step. This branch is based on it.
- `src/sead_sexdiff/data.py`: `run_latent_sensitivity(cell_type=...)` with directory defaults from `cell_type_dirs` (`CELL_TYPE_SUBDIRS` gains `motco_latent_sensitivity` if absent), `check_manifest_cell_type` on the three manifests it reads, `_analyze_cohort(cell_type=...)` for the arm directories, `cell_type` in the comparison manifest; `POSITIVE_CONTROL_BASES` with `run_positive_control(basis=...)` and `_positive_control_read` resolving the analysis and diagnostics subdirectories from the basis, the output path `positive_control/<basis>/<cohort>/` for a non-primary basis, the chain checks extended to the arm's comparison manifest; `render_positive_control` prints the basis. Defaults reproduce today's outputs byte-identically (the synthetic byte-identity check of the previous changes, repeated).
- `src/sead_sexdiff/cli.py`: `--cell-type` on `latent-sensitivity` (directory flags default to `None`, help text shows the astrocyte defaults); `--basis` on `positive-control`.
- `reports/`: one new committed report. `results/` stays gitignored.
- Data: the microglia inputs are on `nixos-home` and the cluster; the astrocyte arms (`results/motco_latent_sensitivity/`) were made on `nixos-office`. The restated control reads both cell types' arms, so the astrocyte arms are copied to the machine that runs it and checked against the hashes the astrocyte report records before anything runs.
- Compute: four CV analyses (two arms × two cohorts) of about a minute each, two `axis-diagnostics` runs, the `positive-control` evaluations; workstation only.
- Scientific choices introduced here (flag and ask before changing): the reuse of the astrocyte arms, covariate set and conditioning on microglia unchanged; the basis registry with `latent_adjusted` as the only non-primary basis; E1 carried over unchanged on the adjusted basis; the predeclared reading of the restated control in both outcomes; the rule that no restated control is evaluated for `extended_covariates`; and the rule that the primary-basis control, the primary analyses and the astrocyte reading are unchanged by anything found here.
